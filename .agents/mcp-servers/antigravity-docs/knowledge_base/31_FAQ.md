Why can’t I sign in with my personal Google Account?
Section titled “Why can’t I sign in with my personal Google Account?”

Personal Google Accounts (such as @gmail.com addresses) can use Google Antigravity on any plan, including the free tier. If sign-in fails, check the following:

Location: Your account must be associated with a country or territory where Antigravity is available. Refer to What is Google Antigravity’s geographical availability? to check your country.
Age: Antigravity is unavailable to users under 18. Refer to Why is my age unverified? if you believe this to be in error.
Account type: Work or school accounts managed by an organization, such as Google Workspace accounts, aren’t personal Google Accounts. To sign in with one, refer to Why can’t I sign in with my enterprise account?
Why can’t I sign in with my enterprise account?
Section titled “Why can’t I sign in with my enterprise account?”

Enterprise accounts, such as Google Workspace and Gemini Enterprise accounts, are managed by your organization. Your administrator must give your account access to Antigravity before you can sign in. If sign-in fails, check the following:

Google Workspace accounts: Your Workspace administrator must explicitly provision Antigravity for your account. If it isn’t provisioned, sign-in fails. Contact your Workspace administrator to request access, or sign in with a personal Google Account instead.
Gemini Enterprise license: Choose Business account when you sign in. Your organization’s Google Cloud administrator must assign you a Gemini Enterprise license or the required IAM role. If no licenses appear when you sign in, contact your administrator.
AI developer tools: AI developer tools may be turned off by default in Gemini Enterprise. Your administrator must turn them on for the region where your subscription is provisioned.
Identity: If your email address exists with more than one identity provider, sign in with the identity that matches your Gemini Enterprise license.
Product: Enterprise sign-in works in the Antigravity App, Antigravity CLI, and Antigravity IDE extensions, but not in the standalone Antigravity IDE.

For Gemini Enterprise setup steps and more troubleshooting, refer to Antigravity in Gemini Enterprise.

Why is my age unverified?
Section titled “Why is my age unverified?”

Currently, Antigravity is unavailable to users under 18. If you meet the minimum age requirement, you can verify your age to continue using Antigravity.

How do I upgrade to a paid Google AI plan?
Section titled “How do I upgrade to a paid Google AI plan?”

Some models require a paid Google AI Pro or Google AI Ultra subscription and are not included in free trials. To upgrade:

Free or Google AI Plus users: Subscribe to a paid Google AI Pro or Google AI Ultra plan on Google One.
Google AI Pro trial users: Submit a request to cancel your trial and switch to a paid Google AI Pro plan, or upgrade directly to a paid Google AI Ultra plan on Google One. For memberships through third-party partners, Pixel Pass, or Google Fi, learn how to manage third-party subscriptions.
Why can’t I use third-party software (such as Claude Code, OpenClaw, or OpenCode) with my Antigravity login?
Section titled “Why can’t I use third-party software (such as Claude Code, OpenClaw, or OpenCode) with my Antigravity login?”

Using third-party software, tools, or services to access Antigravity is a violation of our Terms of Service and severely degrades the experience for legitimate product users. Such actions can result in suspension or termination of your account. To use a third-party coding agent with Gemini, we recommend using a Gemini Enterprise or Google AI Studio API key.

Can I use Antigravity CLI headless mode in third-party coding agents or automate account switching?
Section titled “Can I use Antigravity CLI headless mode in third-party coding agents or automate account switching?”

Antigravity CLI headless mode (agy -p) is intended for direct scripting and CI workflows. Wrapping agy inside third-party coding agents or applications, or automating account rotation, is not supported under consumer plans.

Does Google Antigravity currently support worktrees?
Section titled “Does Google Antigravity currently support worktrees?”

Yes, you can use worktrees in the Antigravity App.

What happens when my computer goes to sleep?
Section titled “What happens when my computer goes to sleep?”

If an agent is running, Antigravity prevents your computer from sleeping.
